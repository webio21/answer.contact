INDEPENDENT BY DESIGN

Payment never buys
a better answer.

Contact Answer is designed to evaluate information and providers on their merits — not on who pays. Your requirements and the available evidence guide the result. Commercial payments do not buy influence over conclusions or recommendations.

THE INDEPENDENCE COMMITMENT

Users pay for analysis, not influence.

Answer Credits pay for the requested level of analysis and research. They do not purchase a preferred conclusion, a guaranteed outcome, or a more favorable provider recommendation. Providers cannot pay for sponsored placement, higher rankings, or preferential treatment in Answer results.

No Paid Rankings

Providers cannot buy higher positions, sponsored recommendations, or preferential visibility in Answer results. Payment, advertising spend, and network participation are not recommendation criteria.

No paid placement

Success Fees Stay Separate

Contact may earn a success-based fee when a qualifying provider transaction is completed. That fee arrangement is kept separate from Answer's matching and ranking criteria. A higher fee never earns a provider a better position.

No fee-based influence

Evidence Determines the Match

Recommendations are based on the user's stated needs and relevant evidence — including expertise, verified offers, service fit, pricing, availability, and documented performance where reliable information exists.

Needs-based evaluation

No Artificial Winner

If the available evidence does not support a clear winner, Answer should make that limitation clear. It should not turn uncertainty into a confident recommendation or hide meaningful trade-offs.

Uncertainty disclosed

Contact Provider Network is a structured source of provider information, not a paid recommendation channel. Network membership can make verified identity, Offers, and documented Deals easier to evaluate, but it does not automatically improve a provider's ranking. External providers may also be considered when relevant and may be a stronger evidence-based match.

CONTACT PROVIDER VERIFICATION

Verified identity. Clearer trust.

Providers and businesses that complete Contact's applicable KYC/KYB checks have their identity or business information verified to the extent of the checks performed. Their verified identity, structured Offers, and documented Deals can give Answer more directly verifiable information to evaluate. Verification does not by itself guarantee service quality, professional competence, pricing, availability, or successful fulfilment — and it does not buy a higher recommendation ranking.

DOCUMENTED COLLABORATIONS

Real activity, not just contact requests.

Where a provider's completed collaborations are displayed, counts should be supported by qualifying Deal records and appropriate transaction evidence or required confirmations. A profile view, inquiry, introduction, or quote request alone does not count as a completed collaboration. A completed count is evidence of recorded transactions — not a guarantee of future results or a substitute for evaluating service fit.

AI analysis, source verification, provider identity verification, and transaction records are different forms of evidence. Contact Answer should label each clearly and show the limits of what has actually been checked.

NO UNSUPPORTED ACCURACY CLAIMS

More analysis is not a promise of perfection.

There is no single accuracy percentage that applies to every subject, question, model, or source environment. Results depend on the task and the quality of available evidence. Contact Answer focuses on showing how an answer was examined, what information supports it, where perspectives disagree, and what remains uncertain — rather than promising an unverified percentage improvement or claiming that AI cannot make mistakes.

A deeper analysis can provide more scrutiny and context, but it cannot guarantee correctness. Review important information and seek qualified professional advice when a decision requires it.
