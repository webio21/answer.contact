INFORMATION YOU CAN TRUST

Clear sources.
Confident decisions.

Trust depends on being clear about where information comes from and what it actually represents. Contact Answer distinguishes information checks, provider identity checks, market estimates, and real supplier offers.

01

Verified Information

Information checked against available sources or Contact records. Where possible, the supporting source and relevant date should be visible.

Information checked
02

Estimated Price

An indicative market estimate based on available information. It is not an offer or confirmed price from a supplier.

Market estimate
03

Supplier Quote

A price or offer actually provided by the supplier. It should be attributed to that supplier and kept distinct from AI-generated estimates.

Provider supplied
04

Best Match

An option matched to the user’s requirements, budget, and priorities. It does not necessarily mean the lowest price or a guaranteed outcome.

Needs-based match
PRIORITY FOR CONTACT-VERIFIED PROVIDERS

Provider identity matters, too.

Providers and businesses that have completed Contact’s applicable KYC/KYB checks receive priority consideration for verified status and recognition on the platform. Other providers may also be verified through alternative reliable sources or verification methods. Each verification status reflects the specific checks completed and the information confirmed — it does not by itself guarantee service quality, product quality, pricing, availability, or successful fulfilment.

AI analysis, source verification, and provider identity verification are different checks. Contact Answer should label each clearly rather than treating them as interchangeable.
