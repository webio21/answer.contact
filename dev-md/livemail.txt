# LiveMail Business Decisions & Architecture Specification

## Overview

LiveMail adopts a **Subscription → Mailbox Seat → Mailbox Configuration → Provisioned Mailbox** architecture.

Billing, mailbox capacity, mailbox configuration, and actual mailbox resources are intentionally separated.

This architecture:

- Prevents billing logic from being coupled with mailbox resources.
- Prevents unpaid mailbox provisioning.
- Supports reseller providers such as Openprovider or OpenSRS.
- Allows future migration to another email provider without changing business logic.
- Supports future Team / Organization expansion.

# Core Objects

## Subscription

A Subscription represents a LiveMail plan.

A Subscription owns:

- Plan (Personal / Pro / Business)
- Billing Cycle (Monthly / Yearly)
- Mailbox Seat Quantity
- Storage Limits
- Domain Quota
- Alias Quota
- Forwarding Rule Quota

A Subscription never directly owns mailboxes.

Instead, it owns Mailbox Seats.

---

## Mailbox Seat

A Mailbox Seat represents one mailbox license.

A Seat:

- is billable
- may be empty
- may later become a mailbox
- may be reused indefinitely

A Seat is NOT an actual mailbox.

Example:

---
Subscription

5 Seats

Seat 1
Seat 2
Seat 3
Seat 4
Seat 5
---

---

## Mailbox Configuration

Represents mailbox settings before provisioning.

Contains:

- username
- domain
- password hash
- verification status

Creating a Mailbox Configuration does NOT create a mailbox on the reseller platform.

---

## Provisioned Mailbox

Represents the actual mailbox created through the reseller email API.

Only Provisioned Mailboxes:

- receive email
- send email
- consume storage
- can login to webmail

---

## Alias

Each mailbox owns:

- one Primary Address
- zero or more Alias Addresses

Aliases do not consume additional Mailbox Seats.

# Business Decisions

## C-01 Subscription and Mailbox Relationship

### Decision

A Subscription purchases Mailbox Seats.

It does NOT purchase actual mailboxes.

Purchasing 10 Mailbox Seats does not automatically create 10 mailboxes.

Instead:

---
Subscription

10 Seats

↓

Seat 1
Seat 2
Seat 3
...
Seat10
---

Mailboxes are created only when the administrator configures a Seat.

Reason:

Mailbox name, domain and password are unknown during purchase.

This follows mainstream SaaS licensing models.

---

## C-02 Remaining Mailbox Configuration

### Decision

Unused Mailbox Seats remain Empty.

The administrator may configure them at any time.

Configuration includes:

- username
- domain
- password

Once configured:

---
Empty Seat

↓

Mailbox Configuration

↓

Provisioning

↓

Active Mailbox
---

Unused Seats may remain empty indefinitely.

---

## C-03 Increasing Mailbox Quantity

### Decision

Increasing Mailbox Seats takes effect immediately.

Rules:

- Apply prorated billing immediately.
- Immediately increase available Mailbox Seats.
- New Seats can immediately be configured.
- New mailboxes can immediately be activated.

Example:

---
Current

5 Seats

↓

Increase

8 Seats

↓

Seat6
Seat7
Seat8

Immediately Available
---

No waiting until the next billing cycle.

---

## C-04 Decreasing Mailbox Quantity

### Decision

Unused Mailbox Seats may be removed immediately.

Occupied Seats cannot be removed.

Administrator must first delete the mailbox.

Seat reduction affects billing only at the next renewal.

Rules:

- No refund.
- Current billing remains unchanged.
- Renewal uses the reduced Seat quantity.

Example:

---
Today

10 Seats

↓

Reduce to 5

↓

Current Billing

10 Seats

↓

Next Renewal

5 Seats
---

---

## C-05 Deleting a Mailbox

### Decision

Deleting a mailbox never changes the subscription quantity.

Instead:

---
Mailbox

↓

Empty Seat
---

Rules:

- Seat remains.
- Seat may be reused.
- Subscription quantity remains unchanged.
- Billing remains unchanged.

---

## C-06 Deleting the Last Mailbox

### Decision

Deleting the last mailbox does NOT cancel the Subscription.

Example:

---
Subscription

5 Seats

Mailbox Count

0
---

The Subscription remains active.

Only cancelling the Subscription terminates LiveMail.

---

## C-07 SKU Upgrade

### Decision

SKU upgrades apply immediately.

Immediately update:

- Storage
- Domain Quota
- Alias Quota
- Forwarding Quota
- Feature Availability

Charge prorated price difference immediately.

Mailbox data is preserved.

No mailbox recreation is required.

---

## C-08 SKU Downgrade

### Decision

Downgrades take effect at the next renewal.

Before downgrade:

Validate:

- Storage Usage
- Mailbox Count
- Domain Count
- Alias Count
- Forwarding Rules

If usage exceeds new limits:

---
Downgrade Pending

Current usage exceeds your new plan.

Please reduce usage before renewal.
---

Rules:

- Current plan remains active.
- Existing quotas remain available.
- Downgrade applies only after all requirements are satisfied.
- No automatic mailbox deletion.
- No automatic email deletion.
- No automatic domain removal.

## C-09 Multiple LiveMail Subscriptions

### Decision

Not supported.

Each Organization (or individual account) may own only one active LiveMail Subscription.

Reason:

- Simplifies quota management.
- Avoids conflicting plans.
- Prevents mailbox fragmentation.
- Keeps billing predictable.

Example:

---
Organization

↓

LiveMail Subscription

↓

Business Plan

↓

50 Mailbox Seats
---

NOT:

---
Organization

↓

Personal Subscription

+

Business Subscription

+

Pro Subscription
---

---

## C-10 Mailbox Quantity Limit

### Decision

Mailbox quantity is calculated per Subscription.

Mailbox limits are NOT calculated:

- per mailbox
- per user
- per account

Example:

---
Business Plan

50 Mailbox Seats
---

All seats belong to the same Subscription.

---

## C-11 Custom Domain Quota

### Decision

Custom Domain quota belongs to the Subscription.

Domains are shared by all mailboxes under the Subscription.

Example:

---
Subscription

↓

Domains

john.com

company.com

support.com
---

All mailboxes may use any verified domain within the Subscription.

Domains are NOT allocated per mailbox.

---

## C-12 Removing Custom Domains

### Decision

A domain currently used by any mailbox cannot be removed.

Before removing a domain, the administrator must either:

- Move all affected mailboxes to another verified domain.

or

- Delete those mailboxes.

Automatic mailbox suspension is never performed.

Automatic mailbox deletion is never performed.

Example:

---
john.com

↓

info@john.com
sales@john.com

↓

Cannot Remove Domain
---

---

## C-13 Partial Mailbox Provisioning Failure

### Decision

Each mailbox is provisioned independently.

Provisioning is never treated as an all-or-nothing batch operation.

Example:

---
Mailbox 1

Success

Mailbox 2

Failed

Mailbox 3

Success
---

Rules:

- Successful mailboxes remain active.
- Failed mailboxes are retried automatically.
- Manual retry is available.
- Support escalation occurs only after repeated failures.
- Successfully provisioned mailboxes are never rolled back.

---

## C-14 Subscription Cancellation

### Decision

Subscription cancellation does not immediately delete mailboxes.

Cancellation flow:

---
Cancel Subscription

↓

Read-only Mode

↓

30-Day Grace Period

↓

Permanent Deletion
---

During Read-only Mode:

Allowed:

- Login
- Read email
- Download email
- Export mailbox
- Renew subscription

Not Allowed:

- Send email
- Receive new email
- Create mailbox
- Delete mailbox
- Add domains
- Change subscription
- Modify mailbox settings

If the subscription is renewed during the Grace Period:

---
Grace Period

↓

Renew

↓

Fully Active
---

No data is lost.

---

## C-15 Mail Retention

### Decision

After cancellation:

Mailbox data is retained for 30 days.

During retention:

Allowed:

- Export mail
- Recover subscription

Not Allowed:

- Send mail
- Receive mail

After 30 days:

The following are permanently deleted:

- Mailboxes
- Emails
- Aliases
- Domain bindings
- Mailbox configuration

Deletion is irreversible.

---

↓

↓

---

- Mailbox configuration is preserved.
- Domain verification remains valid.
- Password hash remains stored.

- User may retry activation at any time.

- Payment remains valid.
- Retry only provisioning.
- Do not ask the customer to purchase again.

# Engineering Principles

## Billing Layer

Responsible only for:

- Subscription
- Plan
- Billing Cycle
- Seat Quantity
- Orders
- Payments
- Invoices

Billing never directly creates or deletes mailboxes.

---

## Mailbox Layer

Responsible only for:

- Mailbox configuration
- Password management
- Aliases
- Forwarding rules
- Mailbox lifecycle
- Mailbox status

Mailbox management never performs billing operations.

---

## Domain Layer

Responsible only for:

- Domain management
- DNS verification
- Domain ownership
- Domain lifecycle

Domain management never provisions mailboxes automatically.

---

## Provisioning Layer

Responsible only for communication with the reseller email provider.

Responsibilities:

- Create mailbox
- Delete mailbox
- Suspend mailbox
- Activate mailbox
- Assign storage quota
- Update mailbox status

Provisioning never performs billing decisions.

---

# Architectural Principles

The application must never tightly couple its business logic to any reseller provider.

The reseller API is treated as a provisioning adapter only.

Business logic must remain provider-independent.

This architecture provides:

- Clear separation of responsibilities
- Easier maintenance
- Easier testing
- Future provider migration
- Scalable Team / Organization support
- Lower long-term engineering cost
- Reduced vendor lock-in

---

# Summary

The complete LiveMail architecture separates the system into four independent layers:

---
Subscription
(Billing)

↓

Mailbox Seat
(Licensing)

↓

Mailbox Configuration
(User Configuration)

↓

Provisioned Mailbox
(Reseller Infrastructure)
---

This separation ensures that billing, licensing, mailbox management, and infrastructure provisioning remain independent, enabling predictable subscription behavior, reusable mailbox seats, clean provider abstraction, and future scalability.

LiveMail separates subscription billing from actual mailbox resources.

Architecture:

## Mailbox Seat Definition

Mailbox Seat:

- Represents one mailbox license.
- Is billable.
- Can exist without an active mailbox.
- Can be reused after mailbox deletion.
- Does not directly store emails.


# Subscription and Mailbox Relationship

A subscription purchases Mailbox Seats.

Example:

Business Plan:

5 Mailbox Seats

↓

Seat 1

Seat 2

Seat 3

Seat 4

Seat 5


Mailbox creation happens only after:

1. User configures mailbox.
2. User completes payment.
3. System provisions mailbox through email provider API.


# Mailbox Quantity Management

## Increasing Mailbox Quantity

When increasing mailbox quantity:

- Takes effect immediately.
- Additional seats become available immediately.
- Prorated billing applies immediately.
- New mailboxes can be created immediately.


## Decreasing Mailbox Quantity

When decreasing mailbox quantity:

- Unused seats can be removed.
- Active mailbox seats cannot be removed.
- User must delete mailbox first.
- No immediate refund.
- New quantity applies at next renewal.


# Mailbox Deletion

Deleting a mailbox:

- Does not reduce subscription quantity.
- Returns the seat to Empty state.
- Allows future reuse.

Example:

Before:

Seat 1 → john@company.com

After deletion:

Seat 1 → Empty


# Last Mailbox Deletion

Deleting the final mailbox:

- Does not cancel subscription.
- Subscription remains active.
- User can create another mailbox using existing seats.


# Plan Upgrade

Upgrade:

- Applies immediately.
- Updates storage quota immediately.
- Updates domain quota immediately.
- Updates alias quota immediately.
- Updates available features immediately.
- Charges prorated price difference.

No mailbox recreation required.


# Plan Downgrade

Downgrade:

- Applies at next renewal.
- Requires validation before activation.

System checks:

- Storage usage.
- Mailbox quantity.
- Domain quantity.
- Alias quantity.
- Forwarding rules.

If limits exceed new plan:

Downgrade remains pending.

System never automatically deletes:

- Mailboxes.
- Emails.
- Domains.
- Aliases.

- Do not create mailbox.

- Keep domain verification.
- Allow retry later.

If payment succeeds but mailbox creation fails:
