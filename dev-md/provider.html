Contact Provider Network — Success Fee

Free to be discovered. Pay only when you succeed.

Providers can join Contact Provider Network, publish eligible Offers, receive inquiries, and be discovered through Contact Answer without paying listing, impression, click, or inquiry fees.

Contact earns a Success Fee only from qualifying transactions that are attributable to Contact, covered by an agreed fee policy, and meet the applicable fulfillment and payment conditions.

1. System Architecture

Contact Answer — Independent Discovery Understands user requirements and independently evaluates provider suitability, information reliability, and matching quality.
↓
Contact Provider Network — Free Participation Manages provider profiles, Contact Cards, Offers, inquiries, and provider participation.
↓
AI Fee Intelligence — Policy Recommendations Analyzes industry, regional, transaction, market, and Contact operating data to recommend fee policies.
↓
Central Fee Policy Engine — Validation and Application Validates, versions, inherits, and applies approved policies consistently. Merchants cannot bid or set individual commission rates.
↓
Contact Deal — Agreement and Attribution Records the customer introduction, agreed transaction terms, fee snapshot, fulfillment milestones, and payment evidence.
↓
Contact Settlement — Success Fee Collection Calculates, collects, adjusts, and reconciles fees for qualifying completed transactions.

2. Free Provider Participation

The following activities are free:

  • Joining Contact Provider Network and creating a provider profile.
  • Creating Contact Cards and publishing eligible Offers.
  • Appearing in Contact Answer search results and recommendations.
  • Receiving Contact Card views, inquiries, and quotation requests.
  • Responding to inquiries, communicating, and submitting quotations.

No listing, subscription, impression, click, lead, or inquiry fee is charged under this Success Fee model. A Success Fee applies only when the transaction qualifies under the policy agreed before the transaction.

3. Independent Discovery and Ranking

Contact Answer must make discovery, ranking, matching, and verification decisions independently of Provider Network revenue.

  • Evaluate user requirements, provider suitability, prices, terms, and relevant evidence.
  • Do not use Success Fee rates, expected commission revenue, or merchant willingness to pay as ranking or recommendation inputs.
  • Do not provide paid ranking, commission-based recommendation priority, or preferential exposure.
  • Do not treat payment history or fee-policy participation as proof of provider quality.
  • Keep fee and settlement data access-separated from Answer ranking services.
Engineering safeguard: The Answer Ranking Engine must not receive merchant-specific Success Fee values, expected Contact revenue, or commission bidding signals as ranking features. Provider verification may inform identity confidence but does not guarantee service quality or fulfillment.

4. AI-Powered Fee Intelligence

Contact should not require administrators to manually define a fee for every industry and subcategory. AI Fee Intelligence should generate and maintain initial policy recommendations using relevant evidence and Contact's own operating data as it becomes available.

A. Analysis Inputs

  • Industry and business-model characteristics.
  • Regional market conditions and comparable platform fee practices.
  • Available industry margin, transaction-value, and sales-cycle benchmarks.
  • Merchant acquisition economics and provider participation incentives.
  • Transaction, fulfillment, refund, dispute, and payment characteristics.
  • Contact's attributable conversion, retention, payment, support, and operating costs.
  • Applicable legal, regulatory, and payment restrictions.

AI must distinguish verified facts from estimates and identify the source, freshness, coverage, assumptions, and confidence of material inputs. Industry averages must not be presented as verified data about an individual merchant.

B. Policy Recommendations

For each applicable policy scope, AI may recommend:

  • Percentage-based or fixed Success Fee model.
  • Recommended rate and permitted policy range.
  • Eligible transaction calculation basis and trigger.
  • Attribution period and repeat-transaction treatment.
  • Refund, cancellation, and dispute treatment.
  • Any appropriate fee cap or other objective safeguard.

The objective is sustainable participation and positive transaction economics for Contact, providers, and users—not the highest possible commission rate.

C. Validation and Activation

  • Deterministic rules validate policy structure, calculation logic, limits, and consistency.
  • Policies within pre-approved regional and industry guardrails may be activated automatically when evidence and confidence requirements are met.
  • Low-confidence, high-impact, unusual, or legally sensitive policies require human review.
  • AI may recommend changes, but cannot bypass policy guardrails or alter accepted Deal terms.

5. Centralized Fee Policy and Automatic Inheritance

Contact controls the fee policy. Merchants cannot set, increase, negotiate by bidding, or vary Success Fee rates for individual Offers. Offers receive the applicable policy automatically from their objective classification.

Rates may differ only when justified by policy dimensions such as region, industry, transaction type, fulfillment model, or eligible transaction band. Comparable providers and transactions within the same policy scope receive the same standard treatment.

A. Policy Matching

Region + Industry + Subcategory + Transaction Type
                       ↓
          Most specific eligible policy
                       ↓
             Validated fee policy

The engine selects the most specific active and validated policy that is compatible with the transaction and applicable region. A more specific policy may override a parent policy only after validation and activation.

B. Missing Policy Fallback

  1. Search for a valid policy matching the region, industry, and transaction type.
  2. If none exists, inherit the nearest compatible, active, and validated parent policy.
  3. If no compatible policy exists, ask AI Fee Intelligence to draft a policy from available evidence.
  4. Activate the draft only after it passes the applicable validation and approval requirements.
  5. If no reliable or legally eligible policy can be established, mark the category as fee-free pending policy approval.
A missing fee policy must never block provider onboarding, publication, discovery, or recommendations. Contact must never guess a rate or apply an unrelated industry policy merely to generate revenue. No Success Fee is charged until a valid policy is disclosed and accepted for the transaction.

6. Fee Models and Calculation Basis

A. Percentage-Based Success Fee

The fee is a policy-defined percentage of the eligible transaction amount actually collected, subject to the agreed fulfillment and payment conditions.

B. Fixed Success Fee

A policy-defined fixed amount may be used where it is appropriate for a standardized transaction type or value band.

Contact selects the fee model centrally. The merchant cannot choose a higher rate for an individual Offer to obtain an advantage. Any policy-specific minimums, maximums, or caps must be disclosed before the Deal is accepted.

C. Eligible Transaction Amount

The policy must define the calculation basis. Unless a valid category-specific rule states otherwise, the eligible amount should exclude refunded or reversed sums, sales taxes collected for authorities, and separately identified pass-through expenses such as shipping. Discounts reduce the amount actually collected and therefore the eligible base.

7. Transaction Attribution and Deal Flow

  1. User searches for a product, service, or provider through Contact Answer.
  2. Answer independently evaluates and recommends suitable providers.
  3. User contacts a provider through Contact Card or Contact Inbox.
  4. The inquiry is recorded with its source and timestamp.
  5. Provider responds, communicates, and submits quotations through Contact Inbox and AI Draft.
  6. Before the transaction proceeds, both parties can review and accept the applicable fee policy and Deal terms.
  7. Contact Deal records the parties, originating inquiry, transaction terms, fee-policy snapshot, and agreed attribution conditions.
  8. Fulfillment milestones and eligible payments are recorded or evidenced.
  9. Contact calculates and settles the Success Fee when the agreed trigger is satisfied.

A. Attribution Rules

  • A chargeable transaction must have a documented, qualifying Contact-originated introduction or other explicitly agreed Contact service attribution.
  • A Contact Card view, impression, or recommendation alone does not create a chargeable transaction.
  • Pre-existing customer relationships unrelated to Contact are excluded.
  • Attribution windows must be defined by policy, disclosed in advance, and appropriate to the industry and sales cycle.
  • Off-platform communication or payment may qualify when the originating introduction and transaction are adequately evidenced.
  • Attribution disputes must have a documented review and appeal process.

B. Repeat Transactions and Renewals

  • Repeat orders qualify only when included in the accepted attribution scope and period.
  • Recurring subscriptions and renewals require an expressly defined fee period.
  • No perpetual commission applies to every future purchase by an introduced customer.
  • Unrelated purchases and independently established customer relationships remain excluded.

8. Completion, Payment, and Settlement

Signing a contract or marking a Deal as completed is not, by itself, sufficient to trigger a fee. The policy must define the required fulfillment and payment evidence for each transaction type.

Transaction TypeExample qualifying trigger
Physical productsEligible order delivered and payment collected.
Professional servicesAgreed service or milestone completed and paid.
Freelance projectsWork milestone approved under the Deal and payment collected.
ConstructionAgreed project milestone confirmed and paid.
SaaS / subscriptionsEligible subscription payment actually collected during the agreed fee period.
B2B supplyEligible goods delivered and payment collected, subject to agreed trade terms.
  • Support partial payments, installments, and milestone-based calculations.
  • Prefer integrated payment confirmation where supported.
  • For off-platform payments, require proportionate evidence and auditability.
  • Prevent duplicate calculations and duplicate collection.
  • Support full and partial refunds, cancellations, chargebacks, disputes, reversals, and adjustments.
  • Generate merchant statements, invoices where applicable, and reconciliation records.
Contact must distinguish transaction records, fee receivables, payment collection, and custody or distribution of customer funds. Payment processing and marketplace settlement must use supported providers and comply with applicable financial rules.

9. Policy Governance and Version Control

  • Maintain a versioned registry of active, draft, suspended, and retired policies.
  • Record AI recommendations, evidence, confidence, validation results, and approvals.
  • Review market and Contact performance data periodically; policy changes must be evidence-based rather than reactive to short-term competitor pricing.
  • Automatically activate only changes that remain within pre-approved guardrails.
  • Require additional review for material rate changes, low-confidence data, and regulated or high-risk categories.
  • Apply a new policy only to future Deals after the revised terms are disclosed and accepted.
  • Never retroactively change an accepted Deal's fee rate, basis, trigger, or attribution scope.
  • Retain audit history and support rollback to a previous valid policy.

D. Minimum System Capabilities

  • AI-assisted industry classification and fee-policy drafting.
  • Deterministic policy validation, inheritance, and assignment.
  • Policy versioning, approval controls, audit logs, and rollback.
  • Strict separation between Answer ranking data and commercial fee data.
  • Deal-level fee snapshots and explicit acceptance records.
  • Attribution, payment evidence, milestone, refund, and dispute management.
  • Idempotent fee calculation, collection, and reconciliation.
  • Role-based access, fraud controls, and merchant statements.

11. Long-Term Commercial Principles

  • Free provider participation and free discovery.
  • No advertising, impression, click, or inquiry fees under this model.
  • No paid ranking, commission bidding, or revenue-based recommendations.
  • Contact centrally governs fee policies; AI reduces manual policy administration.
  • Comparable transactions receive consistent policy treatment.
  • No fee without a valid policy, clear prior agreement, and qualifying attribution.
  • Optimize qualified matches, provider retention, successful transactions, and sustainable net revenue—not commission rates alone.

Final Business Model

Free to join. Free to be discovered. Free to receive inquiries. Pay only when an attributable transaction succeeds.

AI automates industry intelligence and fee-policy recommendations. Contact governs and validates the policies. Answer remains independent. Deals preserve the agreed terms. Settlement charges only qualifying commercial outcomes.

Contact monetizes successful connections, never preferential access to discovery.
