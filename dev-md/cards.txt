* Clicking the Card itself opens the Card details(card.contact/username)
* Clicking a note title opens a pop-up window showing the full note content in an editable multi-line input. Changes are saved automatically. The window includes Close, Undo, Redo, and Delete buttons. Clicking Delete requires confirmation before removing the note.
